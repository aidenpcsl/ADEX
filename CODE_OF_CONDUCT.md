# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the ADEX community respectful and welcoming, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or technology choices.

## Expected behavior

Use welcoming language, accept constructive feedback, focus on what is best for users, respect differing technical experience, and avoid harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, or publishing another person’s private information.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through the project’s private maintainer/security contact where available. Maintainers may remove, reject, or edit contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior conflicts with this code.

This document is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant and is intentionally concise for this project.
